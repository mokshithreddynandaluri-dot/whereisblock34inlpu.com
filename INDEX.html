💡 Our Design Thinking Approach

We designed this solution around the actual navigation challenges described in our INT335 project.

Empathize: Understand first-time visitors.
Define: Identify confusing junctions.
Ideate: Explore landmark-led solutions.
Prototype: Build a QR and photo route guide.
Test: Improve through real user trials.

Our Mission

Help every visitor reach Block 34 confidently without repeated stops to ask for directions.
